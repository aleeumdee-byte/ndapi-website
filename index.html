Nigeria Drug Abuse Prevention Initiative

Building a healthier, safer and drug-aware Nigeria.

NDAPI is a public-interest initiative focused on prevention, education, youth empowerment, community engagement, research and responsible referral pathways.

Explore our programsGet involved
Our approach

Prevention starts with knowledge, opportunity and community.

We work toward practical prevention by helping young people, families, schools and communities understand substance-use risks and strengthen protective factors.

  • Evidence-informed prevention education
  • Youth leadership and positive development
  • Community dialogue and awareness
  • Research, documentation and advocacy
